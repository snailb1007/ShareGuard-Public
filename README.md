### 🛠️ How to join Early Access (Closed Testing)
Due to Google Play's testing policies, you must join our community group before downloading the app:

1. 👥 **Join the Google Group:** Visit the [ShareGuard Testers Group](https://groups.google.com/g/shareguard-testers) and click "Join group". *(Make sure to use the email address associated with your Google Play account).*
2. 📥 **Opt-in & Download:** Once you've joined the group, [click here to opt-in to the test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/dev.snailb1007.shareguard) and download ShareGuard directly from the Play Store.

## 🛡️ Privacy First

We believe your data is yours. ShareGuard is designed with a strict zero-collection policy:
- **Zero** personal data collected.
- **100%** on-device processing.
- No background tracking, no analytics, no remote servers receiving your links.

👉 **[Read our full Privacy Policy here](https://github.com/snailb1007/ShareGuard-Public/blob/main/docs/privacy-policy.md)**
